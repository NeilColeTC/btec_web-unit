# Teacher Notes

## Delivery intention

The three stages are designed to make the development process visible. Learners should not receive the final stage as a model to copy at the beginning of the project.

### Suggested release

- Week 1: expose `client-brief` and Stage 1.
- Week 2: expose Stage 2 after learners have attempted their own CSS/layout.
- Week 3: expose Stage 3 selectively to discuss functionality, refinement and testing.
- Week 4 (if available): use differences between versions for testing, evaluation and improvement work.

## Questioning strategy

Ask learners:

- What changed between stages?
- Why might the developer have made that change?
- Does it actually meet the client's requirement?
- Is there another valid solution?
- What evidence would demonstrate that the solution works?

## Assessment-risk control

Keep the exemplar deliberately generic enough that learners must translate the underlying techniques into their own response to the assignment/client brief. Require learners to retain evidence of planning, iterations, testing and justified decisions.
