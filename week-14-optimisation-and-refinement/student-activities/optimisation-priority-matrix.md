## Step 2 – Classify each issue

Place each issue into one of these categories:

- **High impact / low effort** – usually strong priority
- **High impact / high effort** – important but needs planning
- **Low impact / low effort** – possible quick win
- **Low impact / high effort** – usually lower priority
