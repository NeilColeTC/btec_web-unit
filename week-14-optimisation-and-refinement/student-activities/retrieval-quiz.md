# Week 14 Retrieval Quiz

Complete without notes first.

## Questions

1. What is the difference between an expected result and an actual result?
2. Why should a failed test be recorded before you fix the problem?
3. What does *retest* mean?
4. Why should retesting use comparable conditions?
5. What is regression testing?
6. Give one example of compatibility testing.
7. Give one example of usability testing.
8. Give one example of accessibility testing.
9. Why is a test linked to a requirement stronger than a vague statement such as “the page works”?
10. What evidence could show that a website improved after a change?
11. What is a boundary value?
12. Why is user feedback evidence rather than an instruction that must always be followed?

## Challenge

A learner says:

> “I changed the colour scheme and added an animation, so I have optimised my website.”

Write two questions you would ask before accepting that claim.
