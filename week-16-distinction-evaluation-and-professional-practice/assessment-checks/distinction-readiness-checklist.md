# Week 16 Distinction Readiness Checklist

This checklist helps you inspect evidence quality. It does **not** guarantee a grade.

## Individual responsibility

- [ ] Important project decisions can be traced to my own evidence.
- [ ] I can explain why I accepted, adapted or rejected significant feedback.
- [ ] I have evidence of responding to genuine problems or failed tests.
- [ ] I can explain decisions I made when requirements, time or technical constraints competed.
- [ ] I acknowledge unresolved limitations rather than hiding them.

## Creativity

- [ ] I can identify purposeful problem solving rather than only decorative choices.
- [ ] At least one important solution arose from comparing alternatives.
- [ ] My client-side scripting is an original response to my own design/requirements.
- [ ] I can explain why my chosen approach was proportionate and suitable.
- [ ] I have avoided unnecessary complexity simply to demonstrate techniques.

## Effective self-management

- [ ] I tracked important requirements through the project.
- [ ] I prioritised essential functionality over optional enhancements where necessary.
- [ ] My files/code/evidence are organised sufficiently for another person to follow.
- [ ] I used versioning/backups appropriately.
- [ ] Testing occurred throughout development, not only at the end.
- [ ] I recorded evidence as the project developed.
- [ ] I can identify how I responded when the original plan changed.
- [ ] I can identify one self-management practice I would improve next time.

## Synthesis across design, development and review

- [ ] I can trace at least one requirement from initial design to final outcome.
- [ ] Review evidence clearly influenced a decision or is explicitly evaluated where it did not.
- [ ] Testing evidence influenced development/refinement decisions.
- [ ] Optimisation is supported by before/after and retest evidence.
- [ ] Final evaluation uses evidence from earlier stages rather than generic claims.
- [ ] I explain consequences of decisions for the user/client.

## Evidence integrity

- [ ] My evidence is authentic to my own project.
- [ ] I have not invented historical artefacts, failures or feedback.
- [ ] Claims do not exceed what my evidence supports.
- [ ] Git/version history is treated as supporting evidence, not automatic proof.
- [ ] Missing evidence is identified honestly.

## Final coherence

Another person should be able to follow:

> **what was required → what I designed → how feedback affected it → what I built → what testing found → what I changed → how successful the final result was → how my working practices influenced that outcome**
