# Week 16 Retrieval Quiz

Complete without notes first.

## Questions

1. What makes an evaluative judgement stronger than a description?
2. Why should major claims be linked to evidence?
3. What is a trade-off?
4. What is meant by retesting?
5. What is regression testing?
6. Why should failed-test evidence sometimes be retained?
7. Give one example of a design decision that could be justified against a user need.
8. What does it mean to accept, adapt or reject feedback?
9. Give one example of evidence that could show a development decision changed over time.
10. Why is version history not automatic proof of authorship or achievement?
11. What is the difference between an essential requirement and an optional enhancement?
12. Why can a simpler technical solution sometimes be better than a complex one?

## Challenge

Which statement is stronger evidence of self-management and why?

A. “I worked hard and finished my website.”

B. “I used my requirement tracker to prioritise the incomplete application journey ahead of optional animation, completed and tested the essential functionality first, then returned to lower-priority refinements.”
