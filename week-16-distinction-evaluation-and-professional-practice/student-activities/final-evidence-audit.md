# Final Evidence Audit

## Purpose

Check whether another person could follow your project from client brief to final evaluation using the evidence you have actually produced.

Use:

- **Green:** clear and complete;
- **Amber:** present but needs organisation, labelling or explanation;
- **Red:** absent or materially incomplete.

Do not invent evidence to turn a red item green.

## Evidence-integrity check

- [ ] I have not invented test results.
- [ ] I have not invented user feedback.
- [ ] I have not recreated earlier artefacts and presented them as contemporaneous originals.
- [ ] Claims about compatibility match the environments actually tested.
- [ ] Claims about performance are supported by evidence where made.
- [ ] Screenshots are labelled/contextualised where needed.
- [ ] My final evidence represents my own project decisions.
