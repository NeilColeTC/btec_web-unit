# Professional Practice Evidence Map

## Purpose

Identify authentic evidence of **individual responsibility, creativity and effective self-management** across your own project.

Do not begin by writing claims. Begin by locating evidence.

### Creativity self-check

For each example ask:

- [ ] Was there a genuine problem/opportunity?
- [ ] Did I make a meaningful choice?
- [ ] Is the response more than decorative novelty?
- [ ] Can I explain why the response suited the user/client?
- [ ] Is there evidence of the result?

## Part 4 – Evidence quality

Mark each example:

- **G** = clear, specific evidence exists;
- **A** = evidence exists but needs clearer explanation/context;
- **R** = evidence is absent or too weak to support the claim.

Do not invent evidence for a red area.

---
