## Part C – Relative paths

Assume this structure:

```text
project/
├── index.html
├── about.html
├── pages/
│   └── contact.html
└── images/
    └── hero.jpg
```

Write the correct path for:

1. `index.html` → `about.html`
2. `index.html` → `images/hero.jpg`
3. `pages/contact.html` → `index.html`
4. `pages/contact.html` → `images/hero.jpg`

## Part D – Debugging

The following image does not appear:

```html
<img src="image/Hero.JPG" alt="">
```

Identify every likely problem.

## Part E – Alternative text

1. A decorative background swirl.
2. A staff portrait next to the person's name and role.
3. A diagram explaining a recruitment process.
4. A logo linking back to the homepage.
5. A stock photo used only to fill visual space.

Explain one of your decisions.
