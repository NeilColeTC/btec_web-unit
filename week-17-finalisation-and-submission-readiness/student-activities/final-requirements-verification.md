### Status definitions

**Green:** implemented and supported by clear evidence.  
**Amber:** implemented or evidenced, but the evidence/location needs clarification or a minor check remains.  
**Red:** requirement/evidence is genuinely missing or a significant problem remains.

Do not change a Red item to Green by inventing historical evidence.

## Essential correction decision

For every Amber or Red technical issue, ask:

1. Is there clear evidence of the problem?
2. Does it materially affect a requirement, functionality, accessibility or usability?
3. Can I make the correction safely within the remaining time?
4. Can I retest the changed area and relevant regression points?
