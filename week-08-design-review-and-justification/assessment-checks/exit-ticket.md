# Week 8 Exit Ticket – Design Review and Justification

Complete independently.

1. What was the most useful piece of feedback you received today?


2. Did you accept, adapt or reject it?


3. Explain why that decision was appropriate for the intended user or purpose.


4. Identify one visible refinement you made to your design.


5. What evidence have you kept to show the design before and after the refinement?


6. Identify one suggestion you did **not** implement and explain why.


7. Complete this sentence:

> A strong design justification is stronger than a description because...
