# Week 8 – B.P3 / B.M2 Evidence Checklist

Use this as a formative evidence audit before implementation begins.

## Review evidence

- [ ] I presented my design ideas to at least two people.
- [ ] I recorded who reviewed the designs.
- [ ] I recorded specific feedback rather than only general comments.
- [ ] The feedback relates to identifiable design features.
- [ ] I considered feedback about usability, audience and purpose.
- [ ] I considered technical or responsive issues where relevant.

## Improvement evidence

- [ ] I identified possible improvements from the feedback.
- [ ] I decided whether to accept, adapt or reject each important suggestion.
- [ ] I gave a reason for each decision.
- [ ] I refined appropriate parts of my design.
- [ ] I preserved evidence of the original design.
- [ ] I preserved evidence of the revised design.

## Justification evidence

- [ ] I explain why key final design decisions were made.
- [ ] I connect decisions to intended user needs.
- [ ] I connect decisions to the website purpose/client requirements.
- [ ] I explain why at least one alternative or suggestion was not used.
- [ ] Where relevant, I discuss a constraint or trade-off.
- [ ] My justification goes beyond personal preference.
