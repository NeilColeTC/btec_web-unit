## Review questions

### 1. Purpose and audience
What aspects of the design appear well suited to the intended audience and purpose?


What appears less suitable, unclear or unsupported?


### 2. Navigation and user journey
Can you identify how a user would complete the main tasks?


Where, if anywhere, could the navigation or user journey be clearer?


### 3. Layout and hierarchy
Which content attracts attention first?


Is the order and hierarchy appropriate? Explain.


### 4. Accessibility and readability
Identify any strengths or concerns involving readability, contrast, labels, spacing, form design or other accessibility considerations.


### 5. Responsive design
Which elements may need to change on tablet or mobile?


Does the current responsive plan appear practical? Explain.


### 6. Content and media
Is the planned content sufficient for the website purpose?


Are the proposed images/media appropriate and useful rather than decorative?


### 7. Technical and performance considerations
Identify any feature that may create implementation, performance or compatibility issues.


### 8. Most important improvement
If you could recommend one change with the greatest likely impact, what would it be and why?
