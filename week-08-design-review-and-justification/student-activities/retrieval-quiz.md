# Week 8 Retrieval Quiz

Complete without notes where possible.

1. What is a client requirement?
2. Give one example of an inferred user need.
3. What is a site map used to show?
4. What is the purpose of a mood board?
5. What is the difference between a wireframe and a finished webpage?
6. Why should responsive behaviour be considered before coding?
7. What is the purpose of pseudocode?
8. What is the purpose of a flowchart?
9. Why should image/font sources and licences be recorded?
10. What makes a success criterion measurable?
11. What is meant by a design constraint?
12. Why is an alternative design useful?
13. What is meant by fitness for purpose?
14. Give one reason why a learner might reject a design suggestion.
15. Complete the progression: requirement → design decision → review → ______ → refinement → justification.

Explain why “my reviewer liked it” is not strong evidence that a website design is suitable for its intended audience and purpose.
