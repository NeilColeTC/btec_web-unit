# Learner Activities

These activities are designed to turn the Hot Wheels exemplar into something to investigate, test and adapt rather than copy.

The exemplar demonstrates transferable website-development techniques. Your assessed Hot Beans Web solution must still be based on your own interpretation of its client requirements, audience and purpose.

## Suggested sequence

1. `stage-comparison.md` — compare Stage 1, Stage 2 and Stage 3 and explain why the implementation changes.
2. `design-decisions.md` — investigate technical and design choices and consider alternatives.
3. `accessibility-audit.md` — inspect accessibility features and identify possible improvements.
4. `testing-workbook.md` — plan, record and evaluate systematic website testing.
5. `performance-investigation.md` — investigate image size and other performance considerations.
6. `debugging-challenges.md` — diagnose deliberately described faults using developer tools and code inspection.
7. `extension-challenges.md` — optional independent challenges without supplied solutions.
8. `now-build-yours.md` — transition away from the exemplar and back to independent client-led development.

## How to use these resources

Do not treat the activities as instructions for what your assessed website must contain. Their purpose is to help you understand techniques, investigate decisions and practise a development process.

When you move to assessed work, choose techniques because they solve a requirement for your own client and audience, not simply because they appeared in Hot Wheels.
