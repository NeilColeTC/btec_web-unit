# Design Decision Investigation

Good development is not simply knowing how to write code. It involves choosing appropriate techniques and being able to explain why they suit the problem.

Use the Hot Wheels exemplar as evidence for this investigation, but do not assume its choices are automatically correct for another website.

## Challenge the decisions

Select three decisions from the table and deliberately propose a different implementation.

For each alternative explain:

1. what you would change
2. which users or requirements might benefit
3. what disadvantages your alternative could introduce
4. how you would test whether your decision was successful

## Client-led thinking

A technique should normally be included because it supports a requirement, audience or purpose.

For an entirely different fictional client, identify:

- one Hot Wheels technique you would definitely reuse
- one you might reuse depending on requirements
- one you would probably remove

Justify all three choices.
