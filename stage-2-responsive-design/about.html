About Hot Wheels

Hot Wheels is a fictional creative technology organisation. It exists as a teaching example for exploring website structure, design and development.

Our approach

We focus on clear communication, accessible content and digital experiences that work for different users and devices.

Our values

  • Keep information clear and easy to navigate.
  • Consider accessibility from the start.
  • Test work rather than assuming it works.
  • Improve solutions in response to evidence and feedback.

Development journey

This staged exemplar begins with HTML structure and content, adds external CSS and responsive design in Stage 2, then introduces client-side scripting and refinement in Stage 3.
