## Part 3 – Accessibility check

For your proposed visual direction, consider:
- readable font sizes
- sufficient contrast
- avoiding meaning through colour alone
- clear hierarchy
- simple, consistent components
- image choices that can support useful alternative text

Record any changes you made after considering accessibility.

## Part 4 – Design rationale

Write a short rationale using this structure:

**Choice → audience/purpose → intended effect**

For example:
- Why this colour direction?
- Why this typography style?
- Why this imagery style?
- Why this interface tone?

## Asset reminder

Do not assume that finding an image online means it is free to use. Record sources and licensing information for assets you may later use.
