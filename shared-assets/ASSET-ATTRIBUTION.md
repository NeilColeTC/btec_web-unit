## AI-generated media: teaching considerations

AI-generated assets still provide useful opportunities to discuss responsible digital-media practice. Learners should understand that the use of generative AI does not remove the need to consider:

- provenance and transparency about how an asset was produced
- the terms of the AI service used to generate content
- possible resemblance to existing copyrighted or trademarked material
- bias, representation and authenticity
- suitability for the intended audience and purpose
- accessibility, including meaningful alternative text where appropriate

The exemplar therefore identifies these assets as AI-generated rather than presenting them as photographs or externally sourced stock imagery.
