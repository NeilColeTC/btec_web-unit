## Part 5 – Build comparative reasoning

Useful structure:

**Both / whereas → evidence → reason → audience/purpose connection**

Example structure only:

> Both websites use ______, but Website A ______ whereas Website B ______. This difference is appropriate because ______.

Do not copy the structure mechanically. Your explanation should be based on the evidence you found.
