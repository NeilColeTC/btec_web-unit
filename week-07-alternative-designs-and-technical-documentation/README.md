# Week 7 – Alternative Designs and Technical Documentation

**W/B:** 19 October 2026  
**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Specification:** B1–B2  
**Assessment preparation:** B.P2, B.P3, B.M2

## Curriculum intent

Develop learners' design evidence beyond a single visual concept by requiring them to consider alternatives, technical feasibility, device compatibility, search visibility, digital assets, client-side scripting plans and legal/ethical constraints.

The key progression is:

**requirement → alternative → technical plan → constraint → decision → justification**

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

1. Produce credible alternative design ideas rather than cosmetic variations.
2. Explain how mobile/tablet compatibility influences layout and interaction choices.
3. Plan SEO features that can be implemented later.
4. Identify and record required digital assets and their sources.
5. Represent planned client-side scripting using pseudocode and BCS-style flowchart conventions.
6. Identify copyright and data-protection considerations relevant to their website.
7. Compare alternative approaches against client/user requirements and technical constraints.
8. Record decisions that can later be justified for B.M2.

## Specification focus

This week develops B1 content including:

- alternative design ideas/prototypes
- compatibility with mobile/tablet devices
- search-engine optimisation
- client-side scripting design tools and techniques
- pseudocode and flowcharts
- ready-made and/or original assets
- technical/design constraints
- legal and ethical considerations

## Formative assessment

Use:

- design-alternative check
- questioning against client requirements
- responsive-design decision prompts
- SEO/asset plan review
- pseudocode/flowchart check
- legal/ethical scenario questioning
- technical-documentation checklist
- exit ticket

## Adaptive teaching

### Support

- alternative-design comparison grid
- responsive-layout prompts
- asset-register template
- pseudocode sentence starters
- partially completed flowchart example
- legal/ethical checklist

### Stretch

- require trade-off analysis between alternatives
- ask learners to justify breakpoint/layout decisions
- plan multiple interactive features and compare complexity
- identify performance consequences of asset choices
- require more explicit traceability from requirement to technical decision

## Assessment boundary

Tutor modelling should use a neutral website scenario. For the assessed Hot Beans Web task, learners must produce their own alternatives, technical documentation and design reasoning.

## Next lesson

Week 8 is the formal design review and justification stage: obtaining feedback from two reviewers, recording responses, refining designs, retaining before/after evidence and explaining why final decisions meet user needs and are fit for purpose.
