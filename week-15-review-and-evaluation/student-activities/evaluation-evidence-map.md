# Evaluation Evidence Map

## Purpose

Before writing extended evaluation, organise the evidence you already have.

The aim is to avoid unsupported statements such as:

> “The website is easy to use.”

Instead build judgements from evidence.

## Evidence quality questions

For each row ask:

1. Is this evidence from **my own** website?
2. Is it specific enough for another person to understand?
3. Does it actually support the claim I want to make?
4. Am I relying on only one piece of evidence where several would be better?
5. Am I overclaiming beyond what was tested?
