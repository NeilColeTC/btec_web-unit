# Peer Review Protocol – Evaluation Quality

## Purpose

Review the quality of evaluation without rewriting another learner's assessed work.

Your role is to identify where the writer has evidence, judgement and balance — and where these are missing.

---

## Step 1 – Read one section only

Do not attempt to review the entire assignment at once.

## Step 2 – Highlight the evidence

Find every claim supported by:

- test evidence;
- user feedback;
- before/after evidence;
- requirements;
- implementation evidence.

## Step 3 – Find unsupported claims

Look for words such as:

- good;
- professional;
- easy;
- fast;
- accessible;
- responsive;
- successful;

Ask whether evidence follows.

## Step 4 – Description check

Find one sentence that mainly describes what exists.

Sentence:

Question that could push it toward evaluation:

- What evidence proves this works?
- What effect did it have on the user?
- What limitation remains?
- What alternative was available?

---

## Step 5 – Balance check

Does the section acknowledge a limitation or trade-off?

- [ ] yes
- [ ] no

If no, ask:

> What could be improved or what did this approach sacrifice?

---

## Step 6 – Requirement check

Is the judgement linked clearly to a client requirement, purpose or intended audience?

- [ ] clear
- [ ] partly clear
- [ ] missing

Question for the writer:

---

## Step 7 – Final judgement check

Does the section reach a conclusion, or stop after discussing evidence?

- [ ] clear judgement
- [ ] implied judgement
- [ ] no judgement

---

## Feedback format

Give feedback as questions rather than replacement sentences.

### Strongest aspect


### One unsupported claim to strengthen


### One question about a limitation/trade-off


### One question that would improve the final judgement


## Writer response

What will you revise after receiving this feedback?
