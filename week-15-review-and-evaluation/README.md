# Week 15 – Review & Evaluation

**Unit:** Pearson BTEC Level 3 Unit 6 – Website Development  
**Learning Aim:** C – Develop a website to meet client requirements  
**Focus:** Reviewing the final solution against requirements, testing, feedback and optimisation evidence

## Week purpose

Week 15 moves learners from describing what they built to evaluating how effectively the final website meets the client brief and user needs.

The central evaluation structure is:

> **requirement → evidence → strength/limitation → consequence → alternative → judgement**

Learners should use evidence from design decisions, implementation, formal testing, user feedback and optimisation. The strongest evaluations make justified judgements rather than listing features.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the session learners should be able to:

1. distinguish description, explanation, analysis and evaluation;
2. evaluate the final website against explicit client requirements;
3. evaluate suitability for the intended audience and purpose;
4. use testing and feedback evidence to support judgements;
5. evaluate responsive design, usability, accessibility, functionality and performance;
6. discuss strengths and limitations with consequences for users/client;
7. compare the final solution with credible alternatives;
8. evaluate the effect of optimisation and refinement;
9. identify realistic future improvements;
10. reflect on their own development decisions and working practices.

## Core evaluation areas

Learners should consider:

- client requirements;
- intended audience and purpose;
- structure and navigation;
- visual design and consistency;
- responsive behaviour;
- accessibility;
- content and media;
- forms and interaction;
- client-side scripting;
- compatibility;
- performance;
- testing evidence;
- user feedback;
- optimisation outcomes;
- limitations and future improvements.

## Learning sequence

**retrieve → distinguish → evidence-map → judge → compare → draft → critique → refine**

## Assessment connection

Week 15 supports final review/evaluation evidence for Learning Aim C and the combined B/C distinction-level judgement. Learners should base claims on their own evidence rather than writing generic statements about good websites.
