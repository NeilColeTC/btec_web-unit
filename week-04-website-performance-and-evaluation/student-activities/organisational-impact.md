# Week 4 Activity – Organisational Impact

Website design and performance can affect the organisation behind the site as well as individual users.

## Cause-and-effect challenge

Develop three chains in this format:

**website evidence → user effect → organisational consequence**

Example structure only:

**specific design/performance feature → what happens for the user → what this could mean for the organisation**

Do not use a generic statement such as “bad design causes the company to lose money” without explaining the steps in between.

## Evaluation challenge

Choose one organisational outcome and answer:

1. How strong is the evidence for your judgement?
2. Could the same design choice create both benefits and drawbacks?
3. Does the effect depend on the site's audience or purpose?
4. What overall judgement can you make?
