# Week 4 Activity – Compare → Analyse → Evaluate

This activity develops the writing progression needed for Learning Aim A.

## Part 1 – Sort the statements

For each statement supplied by your teacher, decide whether it is mainly:

- comparison
- analysis
- evaluation

Be prepared to justify your choice.

## Part 2 – Upgrade the response

Start with a simple comparison:

> Both websites use clear navigation, but they organise their menus differently.

Develop it in stages.

### Stage A – Comparison

Identify a similarity or difference.

### Stage B – Analysis

Add:

- specific evidence
- effect on the user
- connection to audience/purpose
- connection to performance or client requirements where relevant

### Stage C – Evaluation

Add:

- strength or limitation
- consequence
- trade-off or alternative
- supported overall judgement

## Part 4 – Overall conclusion

Write a short conclusion that answers:

1. Which site is more effective for its own intended audience and purpose?
2. Which evidence matters most to your judgement?
3. What significant limitation or trade-off should be acknowledged?
4. Why is your conclusion more than a personal preference?

Your judgement does not have to claim that one site is universally “better”. A strong conclusion is contextual and evidence-based.
