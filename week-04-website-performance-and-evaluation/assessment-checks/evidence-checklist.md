# Week 4 Assessment Check – Learning Aim A Evidence

Use this checklist to review the quality of your own Assignment 1 evidence.

## A.P1 – Compare

- [ ] I compare two websites rather than describing them separately.
- [ ] I identify relevant website-design principles.
- [ ] I use specific evidence from both websites.
- [ ] I explain suitability for the intended audience.
- [ ] I explain suitability for the intended purpose.

## A.M1 – Analyse

- [ ] I explain how design principles contribute to creativity.
- [ ] I explain how design principles contribute to performance.
- [ ] I connect design decisions to client requirements.
- [ ] I use cause-and-effect reasoning rather than unsupported claims.
- [ ] I consider relevant performance factors where appropriate.

## A.D1 – Evaluate

- [ ] I reach supported judgements rather than only identifying positives and negatives.
- [ ] My judgements are based on specific evidence.
- [ ] I consider strengths and limitations.
- [ ] I consider trade-offs or alternative interpretations where relevant.
- [ ] I explain how effectively the websites meet client requirements.
- [ ] I produce a balanced overall conclusion.

## Evidence quality check

For each paragraph ask:

1. What exact evidence have I used?
2. What does it show?
3. Why does it matter?
4. How does it affect audience, purpose, performance or client requirements?
5. What judgement can I justify from it?
