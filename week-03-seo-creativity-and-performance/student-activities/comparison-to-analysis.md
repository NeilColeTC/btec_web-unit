## Part C – Direct comparison

Write a paragraph that directly compares the two websites.

Your paragraph should:

1. identify a meaningful similarity or difference
2. use specific evidence
3. explain the effect on the user
4. connect the effect to audience and purpose
5. explain any relevant performance or visibility implication
6. avoid unsupported judgements such as “better”, “professional” or “easy” unless they are justified

## Peer-review checklist

Your partner should check:

- Is the evidence specific?
- Is technical vocabulary used accurately?
- Does the writing explain cause and effect?
- Is audience/purpose explicitly linked?
- Is there a genuine comparison rather than two separate descriptions?
- Is the conclusion supported by the evidence?

## Stretch

Add a balanced sentence explaining a trade-off. For example, a design choice may support one objective while making another harder to achieve.
