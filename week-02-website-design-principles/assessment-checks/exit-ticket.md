# Week 2 Exit Ticket – Website Design Principles

Complete independently in the final minutes of the lesson.

## 1. Define

Choose one design principle from today's lesson and define it accurately.

## 2. Evidence

Give one specific example of that principle from a website you examined.

## 3. Effect

Explain how the feature affects the user.

## 4. Audience and purpose

Explain why the feature is suitable or unsuitable for the site's intended audience or purpose.

## 5. Compare

State one useful similarity or difference between BBC and Amazon relating to a design principle.

## Confidence

Circle or record one:

**1 – I need significant support**  
**2 – I understand some principles**  
**3 – I can identify and explain most principles**  
**4 – I can compare principles confidently**  
**5 – I can justify why design choices suit different audiences and purposes**

## Teacher diagnostic

Look for whether the learner can move beyond naming a feature to explaining **effect + audience/purpose**. This is the key progression point for A.P1 preparation.
