# Week 2 Retrieval Quiz – Website Design Principles

Complete without notes.

## Part A – Week 1 retrieval

1. What is meant by the **purpose** of a website?
2. What is a **target audience**?
3. Give two examples of user requirements.
4. Explain why the same website design may not suit every audience.

## Part B – Week 2 knowledge

5. What is meant by **usability**?
6. Explain what **white space** is.
7. Give two examples of website navigation.
8. Why is consistency useful to users?
9. Give two features that can improve accessibility.
10. What is meant by typography?
11. Explain one way layout can guide a user's attention.
12. Why is simplicity not the same as having very little content?

## Part C – Apply your understanding

13. A website uses tiny pale-grey text over a photograph. Identify two design principles that may be affected and explain why.
14. A shopping website keeps its search box, basket and account controls in the same place on every page. Which design principles does this support?
15. Explain why the statement “this website has good navigation” is not enough for strong A.P1 evidence.

## Confidence check

Rate your confidence from **1 (low)** to **5 (high)** for:

- identifying design principles
- explaining their effect on users
- linking design choices to audience
- comparing two websites
