## Task 4 – Build a comparative paragraph

Use this structure as a guide, not as a script:

1. identify the principle
2. give evidence from Website A
3. explain the effect on its users
4. compare with Website B
5. explain why the difference/similarity is appropriate
6. connect the comparison to audience and purpose

Write one developed paragraph below.

## Quality check

Before you finish, ask:

- Have I used evidence from both websites?
- Have I actually compared them?
- Have I explained the effect of the design choices?
- Have I referred to intended audience?
- Have I referred to intended purpose?
- Have I avoided unsupported words such as “good”, “nice” or “professional”?
- Could another reader locate the feature I am describing?

## Stretch

Identify one design principle where the two websites make very different choices but **both choices could be effective**. Explain how different audiences, purposes or user tasks justify the difference.
